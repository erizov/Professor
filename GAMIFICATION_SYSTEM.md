## Levels and Experience

### Level System

**Level Calculation:**
- Level = sqrt(total_points / 100)
- Example: 10,000 points = Level 10

**Level Benefits:**
- Unlock new content
- Access advanced features
- Get recognition
- Join exclusive groups

### Experience Points (XP)

**XP Sources:**
- Learning: 10 XP per algorithm
- Reviews: 5 XP per review
- Practice: 2-10 XP per exercise
- Assessments: 5-20 XP per assessment

**XP Multipliers:**
- Streak bonus: +10% per 7 days
- Perfect score: +50%
- Early completion: +25%

## Best Practices

### Balance

- **Not too easy**: Achievements should be meaningful
- **Not too hard**: Should be achievable with effort
- **Variety**: Different types of achievements
- **Progression**: Clear path from beginner to expert

### Motivation

- **Intrinsic**: Focus on learning, not just points
- **Extrinsic**: Use rewards to support learning
- **Social**: Community and peer support
- **Progress**: Clear sense of advancement

### Avoid

- **Pay-to-win**: Don't allow buying achievements
- **Comparison pressure**: Make leaderboards optional
- **Addiction**: Encourage healthy learning habits
- **Superficial**: Focus on real learning, not just badges

---

*Remember: Gamification should enhance learning, not replace it. The goal is to make learning more engaging and motivating.*
