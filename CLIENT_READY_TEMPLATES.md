## Best Practices

### Proposal Writing
1. **Be Specific**: Clear objectives and deliverables
2. **Show Value**: Focus on client benefits
3. **Be Realistic**: Honest timelines and estimates
4. **Professional**: Clean, well-formatted documents

### Contract Negotiation
1. **Clear Terms**: Explicit scope and terms
2. **Protect Yourself**: IP rights, payment terms
3. **Be Flexible**: Willing to negotiate
4. **Get Legal Review**: For large contracts

### Portfolio Building
1. **Show Results**: Quantify impact
2. **Tell Stories**: Context and challenges
3. **Update Regularly**: Keep portfolio current
4. **Get Testimonials**: Client feedback

### Pricing Strategy
1. **Value-Based**: Price based on value delivered
2. **Market Research**: Know your market rates
3. **Package Services**: Offer bundled options
4. **Be Transparent**: Clear pricing structure
