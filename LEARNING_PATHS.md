## 📊 Difficulty Ratings

Each algorithm is rated from 1-5 stars:

- ⭐ **Beginner**: Basic concepts, easy to understand
- ⭐⭐ **Easy**: Straightforward implementation
- ⭐⭐⭐ **Intermediate**: Requires some experience
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Advanced**: Complex concepts, challenging
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Expert**: Research-level, very challenging

---

## ⏱️ Time Estimates

Each algorithm includes time estimates:
- **Reading**: Time to read and understand
- **Practice**: Time to implement and practice
- **Total**: Combined time needed

Example:
- Quick Sort: Reading (30 min) + Practice (90 min) = **2 hours total**

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Choose your path** based on your goals
2. **Set aside dedicated time** each week
3. **Follow the weekly schedule** for your path
4. **Practice actively** - don't just read
5. **Track your progress** regularly
6. **Review and reinforce** learned concepts

---

## 📝 Study Tips

- **Active Learning**: Implement algorithms, don't just read
- **Spaced Repetition**: Review algorithms after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week
- **Practice Problems**: Solve related problems after each algorithm
- **Explain Aloud**: Teach the algorithm to someone (or yourself)
- **Build Projects**: Apply algorithms in real projects
